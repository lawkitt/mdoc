# Services agreement

This agreement is made on 3 March 2026 between Harrow & Finch Ltd, of 14 Quayside Lane, Marlow, and Eira Venn, of 7 Copper Row, Tilbury (the Consultant). Harrow & Finch Ltd is represented by its director, Tam Roe.

## 1. Services

The Consultant shall review the company's supplier contracts and report to Tam Roe each month. The Consultant may ask Basil Okafor, the company's finance manager, for any invoice, ledger or correspondence the review needs. Reports go by email to legal@harrow-finch.invalid, with a copy to the board secretary, Mira Castell.

## 2. Fees and payment

The company shall pay the Consultant 4,200 pounds a month, within 14 days of each invoice, to the account she names in writing. Expenses over 300 pounds need Tam Roe's approval in advance. Late payments carry interest at 4 percent above the base rate.

## 3. Confidentiality

The Consultant shall keep the company's information confidential during this agreement and for three years after it ends. She may share it with her own adviser, Quill Abernathy of Abernathy Legal LLP, who is bound by the same duty.

## 4. Договор оказания услуг

Настоящий договор заключён 3 марта 2026 года между ООО «Северный причал» в лице генерального директора Анны Сергеевны Ёлкиной и индивидуальным предпринимателем Романом Игоревичем Карповым (Исполнитель). Исполнитель проверяет договоры поставки и ежемесячно отчитывается перед Анной Ёлкиной.

## 5. Оплата

Заказчик оплачивает услуги Исполнителя в размере 180 000 рублей в месяц в течение десяти рабочих дней после получения счёта. Расходы свыше 20 000 рублей согласуются с финансовым директором Олегом Тарасовым заранее. Счета направляются по адресу buh@sev-prichal.invalid.

## 6. Notices

Notices under this agreement are in writing and go to the addresses above. Either party may end this agreement on one month's notice. The agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.

Signed for Harrow & Finch Ltd by Tam Roe, director, and by Eira Venn, the Consultant.
