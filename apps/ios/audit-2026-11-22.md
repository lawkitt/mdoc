# iOS Swift Codebase & Test Audit (2026-11-22)

Point-in-time health check of `apps/ios/` ahead of upcoming **Cases**, **Document Vault** (storage/files), and **Tabs** organization work.

- **Scope:** Full sweep of `apps/ios/` — 200 Swift files, 44,254 LOC (`mdoc`: 93 files / 21,154 LOC; `Shared`: 27 / 6,314; `MdocFFI`: 2 / 108; `RestoreExtension`: 1 / 48; `mdocTests`: 51 / 12,315; `mdocUITests`: 26 / 4,315) plus `.github/workflows/ci-ios.yml`, `scripts/ios/`, and `.config/quarantine.txt`.
- **Constraints:** Keep the Rust FFI contract (`crates/mdoc-ffi`) unchanged; keep zero third-party Swift packages.
- **Note:** This is a working audit/plan document; retire or remove it once its PR slices land (see `docs/adr/0123-docs-keep-what-code-cannot-say.md`).

---

## Executive Verdict

**No rewrite needed.** `apps/ios` is unusually disciplined: zero third-party Swift dependencies, clear ADR-driven UIKit/SwiftUI invariants, strong privacy/security hygiene in `CaseKeyStore` and `MdocStorage`, and a real-core unit test architecture with no `FakeCore` bloat.

**Targeted refactoring and test cleanup are clearly justified:**
1. **Production code (27.6k LOC):** Two structural bottlenecks sit directly in the path of Cases + Document Vault + Tabs (`Shared/Core/RealCore.swift` and single-document `Navigator`/`DocumentStore`/`AppModel`). Beyond those, there are two genuine god-objects (`MarkdownTextView.Coordinator` and `DocumentViewModel`), one 1,812-LOC mega-file (`Shared/UI/RenderedMarkdown.swift`), and ~10 concrete latent bugs.
2. **UI tests (`mdocUITests`, 4.3k LOC / 26 files):** Bloated and pyramid-inverted. Only **32 of 64** test methods run in CI; **8 more (20% of CI-targeted tests)** are quarantined, **17** are manual/env-gated (including 675-LOC `SurveyUITests.swift`), **3** are device-only, **1** is iPad-only, and **~14–16 active/quarantined UI tests directly duplicate fast unit tests in `mdocTests`**. Consolidating cold launches and pushing model/layout/menu checks down to `mdocTests` can shrink UI CI from **~31 min across 3 `macos-26` runners → ~6–9 min on 1 runner** (~67% reduction in UI macOS compute) while retiring most quarantine/retry debt.
3. **Unit & snapshot tests (`mdocTests`, 12.3k LOC / 51 files):** Strong core philosophy, but needs a shared `TextViewHarness` (currently duplicated across 5–6 files), hygiene/speed fixes (caching ~350 redundant CoreGraphics JPEG renders per run, flushing `SaveScheduler` timers, cleaning stray `temporaryDirectory` writes, pinning a 2027 date time bomb in `ScreenSnapshotTests`), and targeted coverage additions for Cases, Vault indexes, `AppModel`, and `SideDrawer`.

## Part 1: Production Swift Findings

### 1.1 High-Priority Blockers Ahead of Cases + Vault + Tabs

#### H1. `Shared/Core/RealCore.swift` (1,596 LOC) packs 6 subsystems + Vault bugs
Contains factory/conversions (`L9–111`), `StageReporter`/`DeviceConditions` (`L113–187`), `RealLibrary` (`L191–561`), `RealSession` (`L563–1122`), `RealSetup` (`L1124–1230`), `PreparedSource`/`OriginalsIndex` (`L1232–1434`), and `PendingImports` (`L1436–1596`).

Concrete issues:
- **Silent index clobbering on locked-phone / decode error:** `OriginalsIndex.load()` (`L1425–1428`) and `PendingImports.load()` (`L1583–1586`) swallow read/decode errors and return `[:]`. Any subsequent `record()`/`move()`/`remove()`/`markOCRStarted()` then atomically overwrites `originals.json` or `pending.json` with a nearly empty dictionary, permanently unlinking all other documents' originals/pending imports.
- **Main-thread disk churn:** Neither index caches in memory; `RealLibrary.snapshot()` (`L237`) synchronously reads and decodes `originals.json` on `@MainActor` on every refresh. Also `OriginalsIndex.remove(for:)` (`L1407–1417`) writes the file even when the key did not exist, and backup exclusion (`isExcludedFromBackup = true`) is applied in `PendingImports.save` (`L1591–1594`) but missing in `OriginalsIndex.save` (`L1430–1433`) and `RealSetup.saveInstalled` (`L1204–1207`).
- **Reply re-restoration on every review tap:** `RealSession.snapshot(of:path:)` (`L731–736`) calls `library.ffi.reply(...)` and `ffi.restoreReply(...)` synchronously on `@MainActor` on every tap/snapshot.
- **Zombie deferred saves after delete:** `RealLibrary.deleteDocument(_:)` (`L307–332`) and `discardIfEmpty(_:)` (`L334–347`) never call `unsaved.removeValue(forKey: document)`. A document deleted while an unlock save is queued leaks its `RealSession` and tries to re-save on unlock.
- **Fragile `DocumentID` dual identity for Vault & Tabs:** Unsaved imports use `"new:\(UUID())"` (`L299`), saved documents use raw absolute filesystem paths (`L240`, `L280`), and renamed/newly-saved documents keep their in-run ID via `ids: [String: DocumentID]` (`L198`, `L358–361`, `L524–525`) until relaunch, when their `DocumentID` silently changes. Persisted tabs, pinned docs, or vault folder indexes keyed by `DocumentID` will break across rename, first save, or sandbox relocation.
- **Minor lock race:** `RealLibrary.session(path:)` (`L272`) acquires `lock` twice inside one `if let` with a check-then-act gap before inserting into `sessions`/`ids`.

**Refactor shape:**
- Split into 6 sibling files in `Shared/Core/` (no `.pbxproj` edits needed thanks to synchronized groups): `RealCore.swift`, `RealLibrary.swift`, `RealSession.swift`, `RealSetup.swift`, `OriginalsIndex.swift`, `PendingImports.swift`.
- Extract a shared cached `LockedJSONStore<Value: Codable>` that distinguishes missing file from read/decode failure (never clobbering on locked-phone I/O errors), centralizes `relative(_:)`/`absolute(_:)`, and applies `.completeFileProtection` + `isExcludedFromBackup = true` consistently.
- Clear `unsaved[document] = nil` on delete/discard; cache `ReplySnapshot` on `RealSession`.
- Add a Swift-side relative-path (or stable Vault UUID) persistence key without changing `crates/mdoc-ffi`.

#### H2. Single-document `Navigator` + eager `DocumentStore` eviction + `caseFilter` hack
- **Files:** `mdoc/App/Navigator.swift` (`L5–22`, `L68–97`), `mdoc/App/AppModel.swift` (`L22`, `L128–179`, `L215–219`), `mdoc/App/DocumentStore.swift`, `mdoc/App/RootView.swift` (`L101–121`), `mdoc/Library/LibraryModel.swift` (`L83–104`), `mdoc/Library/CasesView.swift` (`L20–23`).
- **Tabs blocker:** `Navigator.destination` models only one `.document(DocumentID)`. `AppModel.show(_:)` unconditionally calls `closeOriginal()` (dropping unlocked PDF/DOCX passwords) and `evictClosedDocuments()`, destroying the `DocumentViewModel` (and its scroll/review/edit/pane state). `DocumentStore` is `@ObservationIgnored` (`AppModel.swift:22`), so a tab strip cannot observe open tabs, order, or working/dirty badges.
- **Cases blocker:** `AppModel.openCase(_:)` (`AppModel.swift:154–157`) sets `library.caseFilter = id` and navigates to `.section(.documents)` instead of a dedicated Case destination, requiring manual resets and leaking `library.query` across visits.

**Refactor shape:**
- Promote Cases to `Destination.case(CaseID)` and move `DocumentsPage` search/filter state off global `LibraryModel`.
- Split `DocumentViewModel.willDeactivate()` (flush typing + save, keep unlocked Original & view state) vs `willClose()` (`doneEditing` + `closeOriginal` + discard-if-empty).
- Introduce an `@Observable` open-tabs owner that drives `DocumentStore` retention.

#### H3. App Shell, Intake & `DocumentScreen` Coupling
- **`AppModel.shareItems(for:)` VM-spinup hack (`AppModel.swift:472–489`):** Instantiates a full `DocumentViewModel`, calls `model.share(asFile: false)`, scrapes `model.share` and `model.errorMessage`, clears them, and immediately evicts the model. Extract a stateless `DocumentHandOff` helper shared with `DocumentViewModel`.
- **`DocumentScreen` → `AppModel` coupling (`DocumentScreen.swift:19, 419, 496–507`):** Reads `@Environment(\.appModel)` solely for `titleMenu` actions (also creating a duplicate Share sheet path alongside `DocumentScreen`'s `.sheet(item: $model.share)` at `L98`). Route title-menu Share to `model.share(asFile: false)` and pass file-menu actions from `RootView`.
- **Main-thread image transcoding (`mdoc/Library/Importer.swift:34–94`):** `Importer.file` and `Importer.images` load full-resolution images, strip metadata, redraw via `UIGraphicsImageRenderer`, JPEG-encode up to 20 pages, and write to disk synchronously on `@MainActor`. Move off-main (`nonisolated` async) and explicitly exclude created image folders/files from backup (`L75–84`).
- **$O(C \times D)$ scans in `LibraryModel` & `CasesView` (`LibraryModel.swift:35–104`, `CasesView.swift:68–73`):** Re-sorts an already-sorted snapshot on every read, scans `cases` linearly per document, and filters all documents per case row. Pre-index `[CaseID: CaseSummary]` and counts inside `LibraryModel.refresh()`.
- **`AppModel.delete(_:)` & toast/undo coupling (`AppModel.swift:24–34, 495–539`):** Does not cancel an active job on the deleted document before hiding/deleting it; split across `AppModel` and `LibraryModel.hidden`. Extract a small `ToastQueue` / `PendingDeletion` collaborator.
- **`SideDrawer.ForwardedEnvironment` footgun (`mdoc/Design/SideDrawer.swift:135–145`):** Forwards only 4 environment keys into child `UIHostingController`s. Any new `@Entry` added at `RootView` for Vault/Tabs will work on iPad and silently read `nil` on iPhone. Keep shared workspace state on `AppModel`.

### 2.3 Root Causes of Slowness & Flakiness
- **Redundant cold launches (~25–40s each):** Multiple methods inside `TableEditingUITests` (3 identical `review.md` launches), `IdentitiesGesturesUITests`, `ReviewToolsUITests`, `LivePreviewUITests`, `IdentitiesHeaderUITests`, and `NewDocumentUITests` cold-launch and re-seed the exact same fixture.
- **Expensive screenshot/pixel polling:** `settledScreen()` (`Waits.swift:69–97`) captures full-screen PNGs at 4 Hz (called 7× in `ReviewPagerUITests`, 4× in `IdentitiesGesturesUITests`, 2× in `EditModeUITests`, burning the full 5s whenever a caret blinks). `caretRect(in:)` (`Waits.swift:131–173`) captures ≥12 Retina screenshots + pure-Swift per-pixel RGBA loops (~10–15s per test in `LivePreviewUITests`).
- **Guaranteed negative-wait stalls:** 15+ `XCTAssertFalse(...waitForExistence(timeout: 2))` calls that must block 2s to pass; unconditional 2s `tip.waitForExistence(timeout: 2)` in `EditToolbarUITests.swift:37`; 2s keyboard wait in `caretToTheEnd` (`Waits.swift:122`); full 6s toast countdown wait in `ReviewToolsUITests.swift:111`.
