/// Guess a manual selection's category without a model, correctable in the
/// popup (ADR 0025): a structured rule covering exactly the selection
/// (evaluated on its line, so labels count), else the rule pack's guesses (a
/// date, a phone, a legal form, a name shape for Person), else Other.
pub fn guess_category(source: &str, range: std::ops::Range<usize>) -> Category {
    let line_start = source[..range.start].rfind('\n').map_or(0, |i| i + 1);
    let line_end = source[range.end..]
        .find('\n')
        .map_or(source.len(), |i| range.end + i);
    let line = &source[line_start..line_end];
    let local = range.start - line_start..range.end - line_start;
    if let Some(detection) = structured::scan(line)
        .into_iter()
        .find(|d| d.range == local)
    {
        return detection.category;
    }
    rules::pack()
        .guess(&source[range])
        .unwrap_or(Category::Other)
}

/// Model files and the shared runtime, matching `check_reserved`.
pub fn model_files(
    root: &Path,
    ocr_root: &Path,
    model: mdoc_store::settings::PiiModel,
) -> Vec<PathBuf> {
    manifest_for(model)
        .files
        .iter()
        .map(|file| root.join(&file.path))
        .chain(std::iter::once(mdoc_import::ocr::onnx_runtime_path(
            ocr_root,
        )))
        .collect()
}
/// Bytes setup still needs for `model` under `root` (see [`root_for`]),
/// including the shared ONNX Runtime under `ocr_root` (`ocr::root`). Without
/// a root, everything is still to download.
pub fn pending(
    model: mdoc_store::settings::PiiModel,
    root: Option<&Path>,
    ocr_root: Option<&Path>,
) -> mdoc_import::model_download::Pending {
    let files = manifest_for(model).files;
    let missing: Vec<u64> = files
        .iter()
        .filter(|file| {
            root.is_none_or(|root| {
                mdoc_import::model_download::missing(&root.join(&file.path), file.bytes)
            })
        })
        .map(|file| file.bytes)
        .collect();
    mdoc_import::model_download::Pending {
        full: files.iter().map(|file| file.bytes).sum::<u64>()
            + mdoc_import::ocr::onnx_runtime_size(),
        model: missing.iter().sum(),
        runtime: mdoc_import::ocr::onnx_runtime_pending(ocr_root),
    }
}
fn verify_model(root: &Path, model: mdoc_store::settings::PiiModel) -> Result<(), DetectError> {
    for artifact in manifest_for(model).files {
        mdoc_import::model_download::verify(
            &root.join(&artifact.path),
            artifact.bytes,
            &artifact.sha256,
        )?;
    }
    Ok(())
}

    config: &mdoc_store::settings::PiiConfig,

# Annotation guide

What a recognition fixture marks, and where each span starts and ends
([ADR 0092](../../../docs/adr/0092-recognition-correction-pipeline.md),
[ADR 0093](../../../docs/adr/0093-flaw-hunt-loop.md)). Gold is
`⟦CATEGORY|text⟧`; anything unmarked must not be proposed. The rules are
the maintainer's: agents add examples, never change a rule (a change is a
`decision` issue).

## Rules

1. **Person** (`PERSON`): a name, or any part or form of one, of a real or
   fictional individual. Each mention is its own span: "Павловой",
   "Ms Bennett's" (span "Bennett"), "М. С.", "Мариша". A title, role or
   determiner is never inside the span ("Ms ⟦PERSON|Bennett⟧", "the
   Employer", "Заказчик") and never a span on its own.
2. **Organization** (`ORG`): a named company, firm or private body, with
   its legal form ("⟦ORG|Northbridge Legal Ltd⟧", "⟦ORG|ООО «Север»⟧").
   Courts, tribunals, ministries and other public bodies are not spans
   (a user adds one to Always). Defined terms ("the Company", "Общество")
   are not spans.
3. **Address** (`ADDRESS`): a street address, from its first element (flat,
   house, street or index) to its postcode or city, whichever ends it:
   "⟦ADDRESS|Flat 3, 41 Larch Road, Bristol BS6 9ZY⟧",
   "⟦ADDRESS|197101, Санкт-Петербург, Ольховый переулок, д. 41, кв. 3⟧".
   A city or country alone is not an address.
4. **Date** (`DATE`): only a date in a personal context: next to born, DOB,
   date of birth, дата рождения, д.р., род. The label is outside the span
   ("born ⟦DATE|14 March 1988⟧"). Every other date stays unmarked:
   signing, deadlines, hearings, statutes.
5. **Case number** (`CASE`): a claim or case number after a label (Claim No, Case
   No, Дело №, номер дела); the label is outside the span ("Claim No
   ⟦CASE|6001842/2026⟧"). Statute, article, section and contract-clause
   numbers stay unmarked.
6. **Identifiers** (`EMAIL`, `PHONE`, `IDENTITY`, `TAX`, `BANK`): the value
   only, without its label; an invalid or OCR-damaged value is still
   marked.
7. **Hidden source** (link targets, image alt text, HTML) is marked like
   visible text.
8. **Never marked:** amounts, percentages, legal references, defined terms,
   roles, titles, job titles, generic category words ("email", "телефон"),
   laws, courts and public bodies.

## Fixtures

Imported corpora are re-marked by these rules: the settlement demo's date
of birth and claim number become gold, so they start as `known-flaw`.

- Synthetic only: invented people, organisations and identifiers; email
  and URL domains under `.invalid`; no copied real document text. CC0-1.0.
- Front matter: `status` (`pass`, `known-flaw`, `model-limit`), `flaw`
  (class), `issue`, `kind` (contract, claim, judgment, power-of-attorney,
  letter, ocr, docx, tables), `source` (`hunt-rN`, `probe-rN` for a probe
  round's synthetic reproduction, `import`, `demo`), and for `model-limit`
  a `reason`.
- A negative fixture marks nothing; its job is precision.

## Examples

Agents add examples here, one line each, when a curator rejects or settles
a boundary case.

- An address with no postcode or city ends at its last element: "Адрес получателя: ⟦ADDRESS|ул. Тихих фонарей, д. 83, кв. 16⟧" (hunt-ru-r1-01).
- A Russian address that opens with its region keeps the region inside: "⟦ADDRESS|Тверская область, г. Ржев, ул. Тихих арок, д. 47, корп. 2, стр. 1, лит. Б, пом. 9⟧" (hunt-ru-r5-02).
- "оглы" belongs to the patronymic, inside the span: "⟦PERSON|Нияз Рауф оглы Иль-Булат⟧" (hunt-ru-r1-05).
- A name split by a line-end hyphen is one span across the break, hyphen and newline included: "⟦PERSON|Верес-\nтова Дарья Ильинична⟧" (hunt-ru-r1-04).
- A staff or employee ID assigned to one person is `IDENTITY`, letters only too: "Staff ID: ⟦IDENTITY|VELK-QUEN⟧" (hunt-en-r3-04).
- A surname-first name is one span, comma included: "⟦PERSON|VALECROFT, Jorin P.⟧" (hunt-en-r3-04), "⟦PERSON|Маркович, Савелий Игоревич⟧" (hunt-ru-r3-04).
- A generational suffix belongs to the name, inside the span: "⟦PERSON|Kaelen Renn III⟧" (hunt-en-r3-08).
- Post-nominal letters (KC, QC, LLB) are outside the span, like a title: "⟦PERSON|Gavira Vennick⟧ KC" (hunt-en-r8-01).
- A surname's lower-case particle is inside the span: "⟦PERSON|Генриетта Яновна де Лаваль⟧", "на фамилию ⟦PERSON|де Лаваль⟧" (hunt-ru-r2-01).
- A lower-case legal form opens the ORG span like «ООО»: "⟦ORG|адвокатское бюро «Валерия Конц»⟧" (hunt-ru-r6-05).
- Name parts in separate table columns are each their own span: "| ⟦PERSON|Кий⟧ | ⟦PERSON|Ева⟧ | ⟦PERSON|Оскаровна⟧ |" (hunt-ru-r2-09).
- Name parts in a passport's machine-readable zone are each a PERSON span, without the filler «<»: "P<RUS⟦PERSON|KRENC⟧<<⟦PERSON|LIDIIA⟧<⟦PERSON|ARSENEVNA⟧<<<" (hunt-ru-r7-05).
- An identifier wrapped across an OCR line is one span, newline included: "Эл. почта: ⟦EMAIL|ksen.rav@\nsvod.invalid⟧" (hunt-ru-r2-03).
- A phone extension belongs to the number, inside the span: "⟦PHONE|+1 (202) 555-0164 x207⟧" (hunt-en-r5-07).
- The title of a work is not a span, even when it reads like a name: «Кедровый свет» (hunt-ru-r3-07).
- An invisible character inside a name or value (soft hyphen, zero-width space, combining mark) stays inside the span (hunt-ru-r3-05, hunt-ru-r3-08, hunt-ru-r3-11).
- A writ's series and number is a court document reference and stays unmarked: «Серия и номер исполнительного листа: ФС 086731924» (hunt-ru-r4-12).
- A notary is a person, not a public body: «Нотариус: ⟦PERSON|Лидия Игоревна Брюн⟧» (hunt-ru-r4-01).
# Recognition correction pipeline

Status: Accepted, 2026-11-01; amended 2026-11-02 (#383). Amends
[ADR 0002](0002-reviewable-local-pseudonymization.md) (ordinary dates stay),
[ADR 0068](0068-short-and-inflected-name-variants.md) (proposed, never
linked; first names) and [ADR 0080](0080-merge-hint.md) (initials).
How flaws are found and fixed: [ADR 0093](0093-flaw-hunt-loop.md).

## Problem

The pinned GLiNER2 model misses what no threshold recovers: neither demo's
home address at any score, no date-of-birth label, a claim number only as a
garbled 0.14 address span, "Марина" alone at 0.29 (#301). The corrections
mdoc has are hardcoded lists in `mdoc-detect` (`TITLES_AND_ROLES`,
`DETERMINERS`, `RU_ROLE_STEMS`, `LEGAL_FORMS`), and a short form of a name
gets an alias of its own: the Russian demo gives one person six (U27).
Fine-tuning needs training data, a new pinned export and requalification.

## Decision

- **A correction pipeline after the model, in layers.** At scan time in
  `mdoc-detect`: model → `never` and `shape` filters → `context` rules →
  structured identifiers (email, INN, SNILS, OGRN) → `span` fixes → the
  user's lists. After a decision, in `mdoc-pii`: propagation. Each
  detection keeps its recognizer (`Model`, a rule, `Propagation`,
  `UserList`). The pipeline targets devices that run the model; the
  no-model path (`scan_rules`) is not a target.
- **The rule pack is data compiled in.** TOML in
  `crates/mdoc-detect/rules/{shared,en,ru}.toml`, read with `include_str!`
  and validated by a unit test, each entry commented. Sections:
  - `never`: titles, roles, determiners, category words, defined terms;
    never a name on their own.
  - `context`: a label proposes a category for the value beside it.
  - `shape`: what a span of each category must look like (today's
    `identifying`).
  - `span`: boundary fixes (trim a title, extend an address to its
    postcode).
  - `variants`: nicknames for given names (Марина → Мариша; Elizabeth →
    Liz), used only for names already in the document.

  `guess_category`'s legal forms and date and name shapes move in, so
  manual marks and the model share one source.
- **Dates only in a personal context.** A date next to born, DOB, date of
  birth, дата рождения, д.р., род. is proposed as Date; other dates stay.
  No date shifting.
- **Case number: a new category** (`Category::CaseNumber`, token `CASE`,
  aliases `CASE_1`; named so it never reads as a saved case). Claim and
  case numbers in a labelled context only (Claim No, Case No, Дело №,
  номер дела), so statute and article numbers stay. It restores like any alias.
- **Addresses: anchor and extend.** Anchors: a postcode shape (UK, US ZIP,
  RU six-digit index) or a street-type word with a house number (Road,
  Street, Flat; ул., проспект, переулок, д., кв., офис). The span extends
  to the clause or line boundary.
- **Settings and the popup.** Settings › Detection › Categories to detect
  gains "Dates of birth" and "Case numbers", both on by default; the
  popup's category menu has Date and Case number; Case number takes the
  next category colour.
- **Propagation proposes, linked when the candidate is unique.** When a
  full name is proposed, applied or marked, its forms elsewhere in the document
  (surname alone, first name alone, first name + patronymic, surname with
  initials, ADR 0068's inflections, the same for first names, `variants`
  nicknames) become proposals with recognizer `Propagation`. A form with
  exactly one candidate full name joins that identity as a proposal, before
  any Apply; nothing is replaced until the user applies it, and a linked form
  restores to the identity's primary wording. A form with two or more
  candidates keeps ADR 0068's own identity and ADR 0080's hint. The popup
  can unlink one mention. A form that is also a common word ("Rose",
  "Мира") is proposed only capitalised mid-sentence.
- **Link signals (U27)**, unique candidate only: first name + patronymic;
  surname with every initial matching. An initial that matches a different
  given name breaks the link.
- **The user's lists.** Keep on an identity adds its wordings to the
  case's Never list (undoing the Keep removes them); a manual mark adds
  its wording to Always. Matching is whole-word, ignoring case and
  whitespace; a person's Always entry also yields propagation's forms. The
  case page shows both lists (remove only); Settings › Detection has
  user-wide Always and Never lists (add, remove). A document outside a
  case uses the user-wide lists only; in a case, the case's lists win over
  the user-wide ones. A wording is on one list at a time: the later
  addition moves it. A Keep lists an identity's wordings once none of its
  mentions is still proposed or replaced. Both live in the
  keychain-protected store, since they can hold client names.
- **Preprocessing is pipeline code**: windowing, table cells read as
  sentences, OCR hyphen joins, HTML entities decoded before detection and
  readiness (`Ann&#97; Petrova`). The threshold and the model stay as
  pinned.

## Consequences

- ADR 0002's "ordinary dates stay" holds for every date outside a personal
  context; the detector's never-Date rule ends.
- ADR 0068's short forms join their name when the candidate is unique, so
  the AI sees one person; relatives sharing a surname are as safe as
  before. ADR 0068's "a first name alone is never proposed" and ADR 0080's
  "not signals: initials" are replaced as above.
- Rules can add false hits; the negative fixtures of ADR 0093 hold
  precision.
- A miss no layer can fix stays a known model limit, listed for the
  maintainer.

                Span::Fix(_) => None,
            })

                            .starts_with(':')
                    })

                        range,

                    })
            })

# Annotation guide

What a recognition fixture marks, and where each span starts and ends
([ADR 0092](../../../docs/adr/0092-recognition-correction-pipeline.md),
[ADR 0093](../../../docs/adr/0093-flaw-hunt-loop.md)). Gold is
`⟦CATEGORY|text⟧`; anything unmarked must not be proposed. The rules are
the maintainer's: agents add examples, never change a rule (a change is a
`decision` issue).

## Rules

1. **Person** (`PERSON`): a name, or any part or form of one, of a real or
   fictional individual. Each mention is its own span: "Павловой",
   "Ms Bennett's" (span "Bennett"), "М. С.", "Мариша". A title, role or
   determiner is never inside the span ("Ms ⟦PERSON|Bennett⟧", "the
   Employer", "Заказчик") and never a span on its own.
2. **Organization** (`ORG`): a named company, firm or private body, with
   its legal form ("⟦ORG|Northbridge Legal Ltd⟧", "⟦ORG|ООО «Север»⟧").
   Courts, tribunals, ministries and other public bodies are not spans
   (a user adds one to Always). Defined terms ("the Company", "Общество")
   are not spans.
3. **Address** (`ADDRESS`): a street address, from its first element (flat,
   house, street or index) to its postcode or city, whichever ends it:
   "⟦ADDRESS|Flat 3, 41 Larch Road, Bristol BS6 9ZY⟧",
   "⟦ADDRESS|197101, Санкт-Петербург, Ольховый переулок, д. 41, кв. 3⟧".
   A city or country alone is not an address.
4. **Date** (`DATE`): only a date in a personal context: next to born, DOB,
   date of birth, дата рождения, д.р., род. The label is outside the span
   ("born ⟦DATE|14 March 1988⟧"). Every other date stays unmarked:
   signing, deadlines, hearings, statutes.
5. **Case number** (`CASE`): a claim or case number after a label (Claim No, Case
   No, Дело №, номер дела); the label is outside the span ("Claim No
   ⟦CASE|6001842/2026⟧"). Statute, article, section and contract-clause
   numbers stay unmarked.
6. **Identifiers** (`EMAIL`, `PHONE`, `IDENTITY`, `TAX`, `BANK`): the value
   only, without its label; an invalid or OCR-damaged value is still
   marked.
7. **Hidden source** (link targets, image alt text, HTML) is marked like
   visible text.
8. **Never marked:** amounts, percentages, legal references, defined terms,
   roles, titles, job titles, generic category words ("email", "телефон"),
   laws, courts and public bodies.

## Fixtures

Imported corpora are re-marked by these rules: the settlement demo's date
of birth and claim number become gold, so they start as `known-flaw`.

- Synthetic only: invented people, organisations and identifiers; email
  and URL domains under `.invalid`; no copied real document text. CC0-1.0.
- Front matter: `status` (`pass`, `known-flaw`, `model-limit`), `flaw`
  (class), `issue`, `kind` (contract, claim, judgment, power-of-attorney,
  letter, ocr, docx, tables), `source` (`hunt-rN`, `probe-rN` for a probe
  round's synthetic reproduction, `import`, `demo`), and for `model-limit`
  a `reason`.
- A negative fixture marks nothing; its job is precision.

## Examples

Agents add examples here, one line each, when a curator rejects or settles
a boundary case.

- An address with no postcode or city ends at its last element: "Адрес получателя: ⟦ADDRESS|ул. Тихих фонарей, д. 83, кв. 16⟧" (hunt-ru-r1-01).

- A staff or employee ID assigned to one person is `IDENTITY`, letters only too: "Staff ID: ⟦IDENTITY|VELK-QUEN⟧" (hunt-en-r3-04).
- A surname-first name is one span, comma included: "⟦PERSON|VALECROFT, Jorin P.⟧" (hunt-en-r3-04), "⟦PERSON|Маркович, Савелий Игоревич⟧" (hunt-ru-r3-04).

- A lower-case legal form opens the ORG span like «ООО»: "⟦ORG|адвокатское бюро «Валерия Конц»⟧" (hunt-ru-r6-05).
- Name parts in separate table columns are each their own span: "| ⟦PERSON|Кий⟧ | ⟦PERSON|Ева⟧ | ⟦PERSON|Оскаровна⟧ |" (hunt-ru-r2-09).

- A writ's series and number is a court document reference and stays unmarked: «Серия и номер исполнительного листа: ФС 086731924» (hunt-ru-r4-12).
- A notary is a person, not a public body: «Нотариус: ⟦PERSON|Лидия Игоревна Брюн⟧» (hunt-ru-r4-01).

[ADR 0068](0068-short-and-inflected-name-variants.md) (proposed, never

## Problem

The pinned GLiNER2 model misses what no threshold recovers: neither demo's
home address at any score, no date-of-birth label, a claim number only as a
garbled 0.14 address span, "Марина" alone at 0.29 (#301). The corrections
mdoc has are hardcoded lists in `mdoc-detect` (`TITLES_AND_ROLES`,
`DETERMINERS`, `RU_ROLE_STEMS`, `LEGAL_FORMS`), and a short form of a name
gets an alias of its own: the Russian demo gives one person six (U27).
Fine-tuning needs training data, a new pinned export and requalification.

## Decision

- **A correction pipeline after the model, in layers.** At scan time in
  `mdoc-detect`: model → `never` and `shape` filters → `context` rules →
  structured identifiers (email, INN, SNILS, OGRN) → `span` fixes → the
  user's lists. After a decision, in `mdoc-pii`: propagation. Each
  detection keeps its recognizer (`Model`, a rule, `Propagation`,
  `UserList`). The pipeline targets devices that run the model; the
  no-model path (`scan_rules`) is not a target.
- **The rule pack is data compiled in.** TOML in
  `crates/mdoc-detect/rules/{shared,en,ru}.toml`, read with `include_str!`
  and validated by a unit test, each entry commented. Sections:
  - `never`: titles, roles, determiners, category words, defined terms;
    never a name on their own.
  - `context`: a label proposes a category for the value beside it.
  - `shape`: what a span of each category must look like (today's
    `identifying`).
  - `span`: boundary fixes (trim a title, extend an address to its
    postcode).
  - `variants`: nicknames for given names (Марина → Мариша; Elizabeth →
    Liz), used only for names already in the document.

  `guess_category`'s legal forms and date and name shapes move in, so
  manual marks and the model share one source.
- **Dates only in a personal context.** A date next to born, DOB, date of
  birth, дата рождения, д.р., род. is proposed as Date; other dates stay.
  No date shifting.
- **Case number: a new category** (`Category::CaseNumber`, token `CASE`,
  aliases `CASE_1`; named so it never reads as a saved case). Claim and
  case numbers in a labelled context only (Claim No, Case No, Дело №,
  номер дела), so statute and article numbers stay. It restores like any alias.
- **Addresses: anchor and extend.** Anchors: a postcode shape (UK, US ZIP,
  RU six-digit index) or a street-type word with a house number (Road,
  Street, Flat; ул., проспект, переулок, д., кв., офис). The span extends
  to the clause or line boundary.
- **Settings and the popup.** Settings › Detection › Categories to detect
  gains "Dates of birth" and "Case numbers", both on by default; the
  popup's category menu has Date and Case number; Case number takes the
  next category colour.
- **Propagation proposes, linked when the candidate is unique.** When a
  full name is proposed, applied or marked, its forms elsewhere in the document
  (surname alone, first name alone, first name + patronymic, surname with
  initials, ADR 0068's inflections, the same for first names, `variants`
  nicknames) become proposals with recognizer `Propagation`. A form with
  exactly one candidate full name joins that identity as a proposal, before
  any Apply; nothing is replaced until the user applies it, and a linked form
  restores to the identity's primary wording. A form with two or more
  candidates keeps ADR 0068's own identity and ADR 0080's hint. The popup
  can unlink one mention. A form that is also a common word ("Rose",
  "Мира") is proposed only capitalised mid-sentence.
- **Link signals (U27)**, unique candidate only: first name + patronymic;
  surname with every initial matching. An initial that matches a different
  given name breaks the link.
- **The user's lists.** Keep on an identity adds its wordings to the
  case's Never list (undoing the Keep removes them); a manual mark adds
  its wording to Always. Matching is whole-word, ignoring case and
  whitespace; a person's Always entry also yields propagation's forms. The
  case page shows both lists (remove only); Settings › Detection has
  user-wide Always and Never lists (add, remove). A document outside a
  case uses the user-wide lists only; in a case, the case's lists win over
  the user-wide ones. A wording is on one list at a time: the later
  addition moves it. A Keep lists an identity's wordings once none of its
  mentions is still proposed or replaced. Both live in the
  keychain-protected store, since they can hold client names.
- **Preprocessing is pipeline code**: windowing, table cells read as
  sentences, OCR hyphen joins, HTML entities decoded before detection and
  readiness (`Ann&#97; Petrova`). The threshold and the model stay as
  pinned.

## Consequences

- ADR 0002's "ordinary dates stay" holds for every date outside a personal
  context; the detector's never-Date rule ends.
- ADR 0068's short forms join their name when the candidate is unique, so
  the AI sees one person; relatives sharing a surname are as safe as
  before. ADR 0068's "a first name alone is never proposed" and ADR 0080's
  "not signals: initials" are replaced as above.
- Rules can add false hits; the negative fixtures of ADR 0093 hold
  precision.
- A miss no layer can fix stays a known model limit, listed for the
  maintainer.

/// One span the model proposed, in document byte offsets, before any
/// correction (ADR 0092): its label from the schema, its score at the
/// configured threshold and its text. The model reads the source's
/// [`View`](mdoc_pii::view::View) (character references decoded); its
/// spans are mapped back to the source's offsets and text. Recognition
/// snapshots are these (ADR 0093).
#[derive(Clone, Debug, PartialEq, Serialize, Deserialize)]
pub struct RawEntity {
    pub start: usize,
    pub end: usize,
    pub label: String,
    pub score: f32,
    pub text: String,
}

/// `raw`, found in `view`, in `source`'s offsets and text.
fn to_source(source: &str, view: &mdoc_pii::view::View, mut raw: Vec<RawEntity>) -> Vec<RawEntity> {
    if !view.is_source() {
        for entity in &mut raw {
            let range = view.source_range(entity.start..entity.end);
            entity.text = source[range.clone()].to_owned();
            (entity.start, entity.end) = (range.start, range.end);
        }
        // A span only over a table header the view inserted names no
        // source text.
        raw.retain(|entity| entity.start < entity.end);
    }
    raw
}

/// The correction stage (ADR 0092): the model's raw entities through the
/// shape filter (`identifying`) and the rule pack's `span` fixes, then its
/// `context` rules and
/// the structured rules over the whole source, all reading it as the model
/// does. Rules win the review's conflicts with the model. Pure: the same
/// source and entities give the same detections, in the order a scan
/// returns them.
pub fn correct(source: &str, raw: &[RawEntity]) -> Vec<Detection> {
    let view = mdoc_pii::view::View::new(source);
    let mut detections: Vec<Detection> = raw
        .iter()
        .filter_map(|entity| {
            let category = category_of(&entity.label)?;
            // Read as the model read it: "%41lice" in a link target is "Alice".
            let text = &view.text[view.view_range(entity.start..entity.end)];
            rules::pack()
                .identifying_at(source, entity.start..entity.end, text, category)
                .then_some(Detection {
                    range: entity.start..entity.end,
                    category,
                    score: entity.score,
                    recognizer: mdoc_pii::Recognizer::Model,
                })
        })
        .collect();
    rules::pack().fix_spans(source, &view, &mut detections);
    // After the span fixes, so each spelling copies the fixed wording.
    let respelt = respelt(source, &view, &detections);
    detections.extend(respelt);
    let rules = rules::pack()
        .context(&view.text)
        .into_iter()
        .chain(rules::pack().spans(&view.text))
        .chain(structured::scan(&view.text));
    let (spans, rules): (Vec<Detection>, Vec<Detection>) = rules
        .filter_map(|mut detection| {
            detection.range = view.source_range(detection.range);
            // One only over an inserted table header names nothing.
            (!detection.range.is_empty()).then_some(detection)
        })
        .partition(|detection| detection.recognizer == mdoc_pii::Recognizer::Span);
    detections.extend(rules);
    // Span matches stand only where they are the widest evidence.
    let spans: Vec<Detection> = spans
        .into_iter()
        .filter(|span| stands(&detections, span))
        .collect();
    detections.extend(spans);
    detections
}

/// "ГКУ «Дирекция района»" is "ГКУ ". An unclosed quote stays as it is.
fn unquoted(text: &str) -> String {

1. **P1 — Public-body keywords erase real companies.** [en.toml:123](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/rules/en.toml:123), [en.toml:150](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/rules/en.toml:150)  
   ORG spans containing `cma`, `sec`, or `commission` are rejected anywhere unless they include a recognized legal suffix. This drops **[CMA CGM](https://www.cma-cgm.com/About/TheGroup)**, **[SEC Newgate](https://secnewgate.com/)**, and **[Commission Junction](https://www.cj.com/about-us)**. These names previously survived. Restrict ambiguous abbreviations and generic words using public-body context; add company counterexamples through `correct()`.

2. **P2 — The geographic-prefix rule accepts arbitrary company names as places.** [en.toml:283](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/rules/en.toml:283)  
   `Republic of` followed by any one to three words qualifies for rejection. Consequently, **[The Republic of Tea](https://www.republicoftea.com/blogs/about-us/about-us)** is dropped as ORG. There is no geographic-name validation. Narrow this rule to recognized places or supporting context, with private-company counterexamples.

3. **P2 — A law abbreviation globally suppresses an unrelated company abbreviation.** [rules.rs:827](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/src/rules.rs:827)  
   In `Data Protection Act (DPA). DPA Microphones (“DPA”) supplied the equipment. DPA invoiced us.`, the document-wide set removes every standalone `DPA`, including references to **[the microphone company](https://www.dpamicrophones.com/)**. Company definitions cannot override the blacklist. Track conflicting definitions and preserve ambiguous company mentions; test both meanings in one document.

4. **P2 — Several public-body and law filters still apply only to ORG.** [en.toml:164](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/rules/en.toml:164)  
   PERSON detections such as `Ninth Circuit`, `Chancery Division`, `Attorney General`, and `Bankruptcy Code` survive: their rejection patterns belong exclusively to the ORG shape, and the widened word lists do not cover them. This leaves the [English PERSON requirement in #378](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/378#issuecomment-6571061364) incomplete. Share suitable patterns across both categories and test each label.

5. **P2 — Public boards remain completely uncovered.** [en.toml:165](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/rules/en.toml:165)  
   `National Labor Relations Board` and `Board of Education` still survive as ORG. Neither the new words nor the body patterns recognize boards, although #378 explicitly requests them. Add qualified public-board patterns while preserving private advisory boards.

6. **P2 — The place list leaves ordinary countries and cities proposed as ORG.** [en.toml:239](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/rules/en.toml:239), [en.toml:268](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/rules/en.toml:268)  
   Examples include `North Macedonia`, `Phoenix`, and standalone `Plymouth`. None matches the list or the administrative-prefix rules. These remain gaps against [#478’s country and city requirements](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/478#issuecomment-6571064909). Add coverage beyond the selected examples without suppressing company names containing those places.

7. **P2 — A line break defeats defined-law abbreviation recognition.** [rules.rs:697](E:/t3/worktrees/mdoc/windows-lane-4/crates/mdoc-detect/src/rules.rs:697)  
   `Biometric Information\nPrivacy Act (“BIPA”)` does not register `BIPA`: title separators exclude newlines, and the remaining suffix `Privacy Act` cannot spell its initials. Subsequent BIPA detections survive, despite #378 requiring defined abbreviations to disappear. Support wrapped titles and test LF and CRLF.
