- **A surname-first name is one name.** A surname, a comma and the
  initials after it ("Penrow, J. L.", "Draycott,D.P.", «Иванов, И. И.»)
  are proposed as one person, whether the model found the surname alone or
  the surname and initials apart; so is a surname with a given name after
  it where a name stands alone, on its own line, after a label or in a
  table ("VALECROFT, Jorin P.", «Маркович, Савелий Игоревич»). A judge's
  "J." or "C.J.", a place ("Washington, D.C.", "Paris, France"), a list of
  surnames and "and" between two people stay apart (fixes #414).
