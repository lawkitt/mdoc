- **Preview: work with Claude Desktop or ChatGPT through the AI connector.**
  Turn on Settings › AI connector › "AI connector (preview)", off by
  default, and add mdoc to Claude Desktop there. An AI app then reads one
  Project you share, its Ready documents with aliases in place of the names
  you replaced, and delivers its answers back to mdoc, which restores the
  names on your computer. Ask opens the AI app from a document or a
  Project; notifications and, on macOS, the companion window show what
  arrives; each Project page lists its AI activity. Stop sharing ends it.
  Any program running under your account can read what a connected AI app
  can, so [privacy.md](docs/privacy.md#ai-connector-preview) lists what that
  is ([ADR 0126](docs/adr/0126-local-mcp-ai-connector.md),
  [ADR 0128](docs/adr/0128-floating-companion-window.md),
  [#1164](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1164)).
