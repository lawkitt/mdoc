- **Comments beside the page.** A DOCX's comments now sit beside its Source
  page at their passages, with replies and resolved threads, instead of a list
  under it ([spec](docs/specs/originals.md)). A narrow pane, a 640×480 window
  included, shows markers in the margin that open a card under the line.
  "💬 N" in the Source header shows or hides them, and "Hidden text (N)" draws
  Word's hidden text in place; both choices are remembered for every document.
  Next and previous comment are Alt+↓ and Alt+↑ (⌥↓ and ⌥↑ on a Mac) in the
  Source. The import note keeps only a partial import; what else the Markdown
  leaves out is a quiet line by the document's name.
