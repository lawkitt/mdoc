- **Projects replace cases.** A Project lives in the Vault and holds its
  documents by id. Move to New Project, Move to “X” and Move to Unfiled
  line a document's names up with where it goes and rewrite its text, and
  a toast offers Undo. Deleting a Project with documents asks whether they
  move to Unfiled or are deleted with it. Unfiled documents keep their own
  names, and the Always and Never lists of Settings are kept in the Vault.
  "Project" replaces "Case" in the app.
