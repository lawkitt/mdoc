- **A recovery key, and Export Project.** Settings › Vault shows the key
  that opens your documents as 52 characters and a QR code; mdoc never
  writes it to disk. After your first Project, a reminder offers to show
  it once. When the Vault was sealed with another key, as after a
  reinstall or on a new computer, Home asks for the recovery key and keeps
  it as this computer's key. Export Project… writes each document's
  Markdown and its original to a folder you pick.
