- **DOCX previews follow Word more closely.** The renderer moves to docxide
  0.21: line spacing, page counts, footnote numbers and symbols such as emoji
  now match Word's export more often. Documents in Calibri, Cambria, Arial,
  Times New Roman or Courier New keep their line breaks on a computer without
  those fonts, through bundled Carlito, Caladea and Liberation fonts
  ([licences](resources/fonts/README.md)); installed fonts still win. The
  preview now knows where each comment sits on the page, for the comment rail
  to come.
