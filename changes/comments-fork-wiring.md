- **Comments counted from every format.** The converters now report the
  comments a document carries beside its Markdown, never in it: the DOCX
  Source cards and the left-out line come from the same reading. RTF
  comments, PDF notes, FreeText and text-markup comments, and PowerPoint and
  Excel comments now count as "N comments not shown", like ODT's
