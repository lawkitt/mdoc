- **A shortcut for the companion.** Behind "AI connector (preview)", off by
  default, ⌥M shows or hides the companion window from any app on macOS,
  as the menu bar icon does, without taking focus from the app you are in.
  Settings › AI connector records another shortcut or turns it off; one
  that another app or macOS already holds is named there, and the menu bar
  icon still opens the companion (Milestone 2 item 15,
  [#1164](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1164)).
