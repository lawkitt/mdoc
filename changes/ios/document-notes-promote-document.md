- **Notes on a document, and AI replies promoted to documents.** A
  document's menu gains Notes, for private notes saved with the document as
  you type; they stay on this device and never go into Copy for AI or Share.
  A saved AI reply, in the Reply pane or a Project's Replies, gains Promote
  to Document: its text, with the real names back, becomes a new document in
  the reply's Project or Unfiled, reviewed like any other, and the document's
  menu leads back to the reply
  ([#1091](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1091)).
