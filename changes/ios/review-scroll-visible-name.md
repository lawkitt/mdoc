  Keep, Keep instead, Next and a swipe of the card no longer pull a name
  you can already see up to the top of the text: the text moves only for a
  name that is hidden, and just far enough to show it; a table scrolls
  sideways to a name in it. After a decision the name could also end
  above the top of the screen or under the review bar, most often in a
  long document with tables; it now ends in view, and a name that stays
  on its card keeps its place while its wording changes
