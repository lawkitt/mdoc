- **The sidebar shows your Vault, and each Project has a page.** The sidebar
  lists Home, To review, your five most recent Projects with All Projects
  and New Project, then Unfiled and All Documents. A Project's page shows its
  colour and name, with Documents, Replies and Names. Names groups the
  Project's names by category, can be searched, and lists its Always and
  Never wordings, which a swipe removes. Its + adds into the Project, and
  deleting a Project that has documents asks whether to move them to Unfiled
  or delete them. Unfiled's Select deletes several documents at once, with
  Undo. Importing a file already in mdoc asks whether to open the existing
  document or import it again
  ([#1091](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1091)).
