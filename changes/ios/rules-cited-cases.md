- **Cited cases' parties are legal references, not names.** A precedent's
  parties before its citation ("Pellam v. Ostrand, 912 F.3d 455", "In re
  Draycott, 77 B.R. 310", "News Group Newspapers Ltd v Brindle [2031] EWHC
  12 (Ch)"), both sides and "Ex parte" too, its short form ("in Pellam",
  "Pellam, supra"), its docket number, "Id.", a cited work's authors
  ("Thanki & Oppenheimer 4th Edition", a reference list's "Morvilius, M.
  and R. Fridell (2031)") and a doctrine named after a person ("the
  Miranda warnings") are no longer proposed. A party of the case at hand,
  named in the caption, after a role ("Plaintiff Ivo Harrow") or anywhere
  outside a citation, stays marked in citations too
  ([ADR 0092](docs/adr/0092-recognition-correction-pipeline.md), #561).
