- **Delete can be undone; a narrow iPad gets the drawer.** Deleting a
  document from the sidebar, the Documents page or its title menu shows
  "Deleted … · Undo", and the document goes only when the toast does or
  mdoc leaves the screen. On an iPad in portrait or a narrow window the
  sidebar slides over from ☰ as on iPhone, and ⌘N and ⌘K are in the list
  of shortcuts that holding ⌘ shows
