- **Dotless initials after a person's role are a person.** In a
  depersonalised Russian act, two or three capitals after a role such as
  «потерпевший», «гр.», «ИП» or «свидетель» («Потерпевший КЛМ», «ИП ДРС»)
  are proposed as a person, not an organization, and so are their repeats.
  Letters the document also writes as a company's («ООО «ДРС»», «АО БАВ»),
  abbreviations («ООО», «УФК», «РФ») and headings in capitals stay as they
  were (fixes #491).
