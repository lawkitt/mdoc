- **Dotless initials after a person's role are a person.** In a
  depersonalised Russian act, two or three capitals after a role only a
  person has, such as «потерпевший», «гр.», «ИП» or «свидетель»
  («Потерпевший КЛМ», «ИП ДРС»), are proposed as a person, not an
  organization, and so are their repeats. After a party's role («истец»,
  «ответчик») only letters the model took for an organization become the
  person. Companies («ООО «ДРС»», «АО БАВ», «(далее – КСС)»), public
  bodies («МЧС»), abbreviations, letters before a capitalised word
  («гражданин РК») and headings in capitals stay as they were (fixes #491).
