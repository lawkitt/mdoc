- **Edit mode keeps your place.** The pencil, Done and the Text/Original
  switch keep the text where you were reading, the caret and a selection;
  with nothing selected the keyboard waits for a tap in the text. Done is
  a checkmark in its own capsule, Identities are hidden while editing, and
  a long title is cut at its end
  ([ADR 0110](docs/adr/0110-mobile-editor-polish.md)).
