- **A surname keeps its particle, and a short name after «гражданин» or
  "Mr" is a name.** A lowercase particle before a proposed surname joins
  it ("де Лаваль", "фон дер Пален", "van der Berg", "de la Cruz",
  "d'Estaing", «аль-Хадид»), with the given name and patronymic or initials
  before it, and its repeats keep it; «да», «фон», "van" and "ten", which
  are also words, join only after a name, a title or a word like «на
  фамилию». Two short capitalised words after «гражданин», «господин» or a
  courtesy title are proposed as a person («гражданина Лю Бо», "Mr Li
  Na"); a country after «гражданин» stays unmarked (fixes #403, fixes
  #404).
