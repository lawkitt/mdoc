- **Names are proposed with the right boundaries.** An organization keeps
  its legal form and quotes ("ООО «Северный Вектор»") and loses the
  abbreviation's full stop ("Northbridge Legal Ltd"); a title stays out of a
  person's name ("Ms Bennett" proposes "Bennett"), and initials after a
  surname join it ("Ветлин Н. О.")
  ([ADR 0092](docs/adr/0092-recognition-correction-pipeline.md), fixes
  #374, #382).
