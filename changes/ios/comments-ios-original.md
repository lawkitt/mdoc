- **DOCX files show their original page, with comments.** The Original pane
  renders a Word file on the phone, as desktop's preview does, and deletes the
  rendering when you leave it. Each comment is a marker in the page margin
  that opens its thread in a sheet; an iPad in landscape shows the comments
  as cards beside the page. "Comments" hides or shows them, VoiceOver reads
  each marker after its line, and the Comments rotor steps through them.
  What the text leaves out, such as pictures, is now a quiet line under the
  title; the orange banner is kept for pages OCR skipped.
