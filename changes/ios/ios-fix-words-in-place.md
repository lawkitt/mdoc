- **Fix the words in place.** While a name's words are fixed the text is
  plain, with one tinted selection whose handles behave as any selection's,
  and the bar at the bottom holds the only Hide «…»; a press on the name no
  longer shows its raw link with Open, Copy and Share, in any state. A name
  in a table cell shows whole and its table holds still meanwhile. On the
  review card Fix the words moves into the alias chip's menu, and the chip
  row is clipped at the card's edges
  ([ADR 0115](docs/adr/0115-ios-drawer-close-fix-words-in-place-identities-header.md)).
