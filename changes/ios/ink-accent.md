- **Ink instead of teal.** The accent is near-black in light mode and
  near-white in dark mode, like the app icon, with white or black words on
  it, so dark mode's filled buttons read clearly; text buttons such as
  Select, Not now and Undo are the text colour at medium weight, and the
  name under review keeps its category colour with an ink outline
