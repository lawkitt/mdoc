- **Edit a document's text.** With Show Markdown on, Edit (⌘E) on the
  document screen opens edit mode: typing saves once it pauses, on leaving
  the document and when mdoc leaves the screen; ⌘Z and the review bar's
  Undo take typing back beside review decisions; proposals follow the
  edits and names typed again are proposed after a pause; Cut and Copy stay
  inside mdoc; the find bar replaces text
  ([ADR 0109](docs/adr/0109-mobile-markdown-editor.md)).
