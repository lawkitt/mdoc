- **Not a name.** A name found by mistake, or one you'd rather leave
  alone, can now come off with no trace: Not a name, at the end of the
  card's category menu, a name's long-press menu, an Identities row's
  menu and Select's new ⋯, unmarks it in the scope shown. Replaced text
  returns to its original, nothing in the text, the review bar or
  Identities shows it, Find names again doesn't propose it, and the
  next save takes its wording out of the case unless another document
  still uses its alias. Undo brings it back
  ([ADR 0122](docs/adr/0122-not-a-name.md)).
