- **The Original is just the page.** On iPhone, switching to Original puts
  away the review card, Review / Replace and Identities, since nothing there
  acts on names; Text brings them back as they were. A running job's
  progress stays. The page runs to the bottom of the screen.
- **Zoom that stays put.** A PDF or Word page no longer zooms out below its
  width, and zooms in to five times. A double-tap toggles fit and 2×. One
  round button brings a zoomed page back to fit, or, past the first page,
  back to the top; the text gets the same ↑ after a screen of scrolling,
  and tapping the status bar scrolls to the top again.
