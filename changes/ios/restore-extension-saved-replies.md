- **Restore with mdoc saves replies in the Vault, and the app lists them.**
  The extension restores with the Project or Unfiled document of the last
  Copy for AI or Share, and its picker lists Projects, then Unfiled
  documents. Save keeps the reply in the Vault. A document's Reply pane lists
  the saved replies that restore with its names, newest first, and Remove
  deletes the one shown. A Project's page lists its replies, and a tap shows
  one restored. The app picks up a reply saved in the extension when it comes
  back to the foreground, when a list is pulled, or when its share sheet
  closes ([#1091](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1091)).
