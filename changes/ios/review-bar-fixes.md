- **The review bar keeps the name in view and swipes like a carousel.**
  Stepping to a name far down a long document scrolls it into view above
  the bar every time, in a table too; the card sits in a strip beside its
  neighbors, follows the finger and settles on the next or previous card
  past a third of its width or with a fling, the same both ways; the
  compact bar keeps one height whether or not the name repeats; and with
  Identities open, a swipe from the left edge closes Identities instead
  of opening the sidebar.
