- **A sidebar, as in the Claude app.** Swipe from the left edge or tap ☰
  and the screen slides aside: search, Cases, Documents, the documents to
  review with how many names are left, and the recent ones, with Settings
  and New at the bottom. With nothing open, Start offers the document to
  continue and the ways to add one. ⌘K searches and ⌘N starts something
  new on a keyboard ([ADR 0102](docs/adr/0102-mobile-sidebar.md)).
