- **The review bar follows your finger.** Drag the grabber, the progress
  line or the card up for every mention of the name and down again; it
  settles compact or at about 60% of the screen, and only × ends review.
  Each mention shows its heading and the original name highlighted in its
  own sentence, with a round mark that replaces or keeps just that one. A
  decided card offers Keep instead and Next; "All 15 reviewed" sums up
  above Share. Tips are now one quiet line in the bar, gone once used
  ([ADR 0101](docs/adr/0101-mobile-review-bar-polish.md)).
