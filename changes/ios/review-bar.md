- **Review in a bar, with undo.** Tapping a name opens a compact bar at
  the bottom instead of a sheet; the text stays in view and scrolls, and
  swiping the card steps through the names. Keep and Replace move on to
  the next one, Undo (or shake) takes a step back, and chips change the
  category, rename the alias, say a name is the same as another, split it
  off or set who a contact belongs to. Hide name on a selection makes a
  proposal; Find in document marks a word the scan missed; Identities
  gains search, a Show filter, mention rows, swipe and long-press actions,
  Review this identity, and dragging one name onto another
  ([ADR 0098](docs/adr/0098-mobile-review-bar.md)).
