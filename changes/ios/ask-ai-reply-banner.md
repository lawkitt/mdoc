- **Ask AI and "Paste reply?" (preview).** With Settings › AI connector
  (preview) on, a Ready document's Ask AI shares its text, names replaced
  and titled by the file's pseudonymized name, into an AI app through the
  share sheet. Back in mdoc, a "Paste reply?" banner restores the answer in
  one tap, and Type a reply stays. The preview is off by default
  ([#1164](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1164)).
