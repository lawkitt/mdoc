- **Adjust Words starts and ends cleanly.** Chosen from a name's
  long-press menu, Adjust Words shows the name's words once the menu has
  closed, in one frame: the alias and the words under it are no longer
  drawn over each other. With a name in a table cell, a tap in another
  cell or a press in the text now ends Adjust Words as Cancel does, and
  the cell shows its alias again, instead of leaving the bar up over
  nothing
