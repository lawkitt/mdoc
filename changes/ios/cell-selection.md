- **Selecting text in a table cell works as in the rest of the text.**
  Dragging a selection handle in a cell moves the highlight with the
  finger, the handles stay on its ends and the loupe on the handle; the
  selection no longer flickers or turns the whole table grey. It stays in
  its cell: past the cell's edge the handle stops at the end of the line
  or of the cell, and Select All in a cell selects that cell's text
  ([ADR 0110](docs/adr/0110-mobile-editor-polish.md)).
