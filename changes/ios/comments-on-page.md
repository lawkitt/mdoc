- **Comments sit quietly on the page.** The "Comments" bar over a Word
  file's original is gone: Show comments, with how many there are, and
  "Not on the page" are in the document's menu beside Show Markdown.
  Markers are small yellow dots in the page's margin, clear of the text,
  and the commented passages are a softer yellow. A double-tap on a
  passage zooms; a single tap opens its thread.
