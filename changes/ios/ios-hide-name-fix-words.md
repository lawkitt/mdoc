  name's span.** A selection outside any name gets a tinted highlight and a
  menu reading Hide "…" beside Hide as ›, the categories with the guess
  first; the selection snaps to whole words and drops the punctuation at
  its ends. Fix the words, in a name's long-press menu and on the review
