- **Notes on a document, and AI replies promoted to documents.** Each
  document's Inspector gains a Notes tab for private notes, saved as you type
  and sealed with the document; they never go into Copy for AI, Share or the
  shelf, and Export Project writes them as `<Title>.notes.md` beside the
  document. A saved AI reply's page gains Promote to Document: its text, with
  the real names back, becomes a new document in the reply's Project or
  Unfiled, reviewed like any other, and the document links back to the reply
  ([#1091](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1091)).
