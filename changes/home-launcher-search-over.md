- **Home is a launcher, and search finds your documents.** Home keeps the
  drop zone and its quick actions, New document among them, and adds a
  Continue strip of documents that need review and saved replies; it no
  longer lists Projects or recent files. Search (⌘K / Ctrl+K) finds documents
  by title or by their Project's name, and Projects by name. Pseudonymize
  clipboard never adds a document.
