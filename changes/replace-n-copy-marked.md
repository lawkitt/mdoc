- **Copying a selection says what it holds.** The right-click menu offers
  "Replace N and Copy" when names in a selection aren't replaced yet: it
  replaces them as one undo step and copies the result. A copy that mdoc
  can't confirm is clean is marked on the clipboard, as a restored reply's
  copy is. This applies in Edit, Original, Pseudonymized and Side by side,
  and to Copy Markdown and to a comment card's text. ⌘C still copies at
  once, with no notice
  ([#1209](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1209)).
