- **Project page and saved AI replies.** A Project's page has a header
  with its colour, name, counts, Add document and a ⋯ menu (Rename, Colour,
  Categories to detect, Export Project…, Delete Project…), and three
  views: Documents, Replies and Names, where names are searchable, grouped
  by category and shown with the Always and Never lists. An AI reply pasted
  into a document's reply box, or with Paste AI Reply, is saved to the
  Vault as received and opens on its own page with the real names restored
  from the people it named, even after its document moves or a name is
  renumbered. Share lists a document's saved replies, and Paste AI Reply no
  longer opens an editable restored tab.
