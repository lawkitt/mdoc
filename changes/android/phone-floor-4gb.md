- **Find names on phones with 4 GB or more.** The name finder now needs
  4 GB of memory instead of 8 GB. A scan has no time limit: it shows the
  minutes it has left, starts only with enough memory free, and pauses while
  the phone is too hot. After the name finder installs, mdoc times it once;
  a long document shows "About N min on this phone" before its scan, and
  Settings shows the speed per 10 pages. A phone too slow for 10,000
  characters in 10 minutes still marks names by selection (#907).
