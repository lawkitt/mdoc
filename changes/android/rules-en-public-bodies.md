- **English courts, public bodies, laws and place names are no longer
  proposed as organizations or people.** Departments, offices, agencies,
  bureaus, commissions, Congress, circuits ("9th Cir."), tribunals' short
  names ("HMRC", "EAT"), Acts, amendments, codes ("18 U.S.C.") and an
  abbreviation defined beside one ("… Privacy Act (“BIPA”)") are dropped,
  and so are a country, US state or major city alone, "State", "States"
  and "Federal" alone, and a place after "State of" or "Town of" ("State
  of …", "Town of …"); companies by their legal form ("… LLC", "… Corp.")
  stay whole ([ADR 0092](docs/adr/0092-recognition-correction-pipeline.md),
  #378, #478).
