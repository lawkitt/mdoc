- **A company's short name is no person.** A name the detector took for a
  person is proposed as the company where the document has that company:
  a short name it defines ("Quillon Freight Corp. (“Quillon”)"), the name
  before a legal form or "Bank" ("Pellam" of "Pellam Bank"), or the whole
  name in another letter case. A name with "airlines" or "air fares" after
  it, and a team "known as" one, is a company too; a person who shares
  the word, with a title or a given name, stays a person (part of #548).
