- **A sidebar shows your Vault, and each Project has a page.** The sidebar
  lists Home, To review, your five most recent Projects with All Projects
  and New Project, then Unfiled and All Documents. Home shows the document
  to continue. A Project's page shows its color and name, with Documents,
  Replies and Names. Names groups the Project's names by category, can be
  searched, and lists its Always and Never wordings, each with Remove. Its
  Add adds into the Project, and deleting a Project that has documents asks
  whether to move them to Unfiled or delete them. Unfiled's Select deletes
  several documents at once, and every delete from a list offers Undo.
  Importing a file already in mdoc asks whether to open the existing
  document or import it again
  ([#1091](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1091)).
