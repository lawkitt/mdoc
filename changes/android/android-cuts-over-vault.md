- **Documents live in the encrypted Vault, and cases are Projects.** A scan,
  file, photo or paste lands in Unfiled, or in the Project picked when adding
  it; no import makes a Project of its own. Documents are saved and reopened
  by id with their review, an original is viewed from the Vault and wiped when
  the document closes, Copy and Share check that a document is still ready
  against its Project's names, and a phone without the name finder can share a
  document once every name is decided. Restore with mdoc restores with the
  place shared from last and saves the reply in the Vault, where the document
  shows it restored. The old library's files stay untouched and no longer
  show: restore outstanding replies before updating
  ([#1091](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1091)).
