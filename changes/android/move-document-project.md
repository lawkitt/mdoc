- **Move a document to a Project.** A document's menu and the menus of
  Unfiled, All Documents and a Project's page offer Move to Project…, Move to
  New Project… and, from a Project, Move to Unfiled, as iOS does. The
  document's names take the aliases of the place it joins and its text is
  rewritten with them; "Moved to Riverside" offers Undo, which moves it back.
  An open document follows its move, and one that stops part way opens again
  where the Vault has it, so it keeps saving
  ([#1091](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1091)).
