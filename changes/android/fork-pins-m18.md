- **A sentence that matches the header stays on a short page, even right
  under the header.** When a page with only a line or two shows the
  running header where the other pages have it and the same sentence again
  just below, only the header is removed now, and only it is counted; the
  sentence stays. It also holds for repeated footers. A PDF whose text
  recognition was interrupted before this update reads its pages again
  ([#925](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/925)).
