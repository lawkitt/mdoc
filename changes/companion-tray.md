- **The companion window on Windows, from a tray icon.** Behind "AI
  connector (preview)", off by default, Windows gets a notification-area
  icon that toggles the companion window (a click, or Enter or Space) and
  offers Open mdoc, Settings and Quit (a right click). As on macOS the
  companion never takes focus from the app in front, is left out of screen
  capture, and hides the restored reply until the pointer is over it; its
  rows copy and open but don't drag out yet. With Settings › General ›
  "Keep mdoc in the system tray" on, the default, closing the window hides
  it in the tray; the first time, a note says so. With the preview off
  Windows is as before (Milestone 2 item 17,
  [#1164](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1164)).
