- **The AI connector's deliveries and AI activity.** Behind "AI connector
  (preview)", off by default, the connector service takes an AI app's
  answer back. It lands as an AI Reply of the shared Project, restored by
  the identities its aliases meant, or as a new unreviewed Document
  promoted from that reply. A delivery must name documents the app read
  since the Project was shared, and is refused for a document in another
  Project, Unfiled, deleted or excluded from AI. It answers with ids and
  counts only; nothing restored goes back to the app. Each read and
  delivery is recorded in the Vault with its app and time, for the
  Project page's AI activity list. Nothing of it runs or shows yet
  (Milestone 2 item 6, [#1164](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1164)).
