- **The Vault tree is the navigation.** The sidebar has no Open section or
  tabs any more: Search, Home, then Projects, which start collapsed,
  expand to all their documents and remember it, then Unfiled with its 7
  newest imports and Show all. Drag a document onto a Project or Unfiled
  to move it; select Unfiled documents to delete them together. The 8
  documents viewed last stay alive with their undo and scroll, `⌃Tab`
  (`Ctrl+Tab` on Windows) switches between them by recency, and `⌘W`
  returns to Home ([#1091](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1091)).
