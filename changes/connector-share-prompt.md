- **The AI project in the window, the share prompt and AI activity.**
  Behind "AI connector (preview)", off by default, the sidebar always shows
  which Project AI apps may read, with Stop sharing, and so does that
  Project's page. When an AI app asks while nothing is shared, a card
  offers to share the Project viewed last, or another, and only its Share
  button shares: moving between documents and Projects never changes what
  is shared. A document entering the shared Project is scanned in its tab
  as Pseudonymize would scan it, proposing names and replacing none. The
  Project page gains an "AI activity" list of which app read which
  document and what it delivered, and when (Milestone 2 item 8,
  [#1164](https://github.com/lawkitt/mdoc/issues/1164)).
