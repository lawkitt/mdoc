- **PDF annotations beside the page.** A PDF's sticky notes, FreeText boxes
  and commented highlights, underlines, strikeouts and squiggles now sit on
  the same comment rail and markers as a DOCX's comments, at their place on
  the page, rotated and cropped pages included, with replies and resolved
  notes ([spec](docs/specs/originals.md)). mdoc tints the marked words and
  leaves out the PDF's own note icons, popups and boxes, so nothing draws
  twice; stamps, ink, form fields and highlights without a comment stay as
  the PDF draws them. Annotations the rail shows no longer count in the
  left-out line.
